# PERSONAL BRAND POSITIONING

Status: Active v2.0  
Purpose: Define the public-facing commercial identity of the maker above Stranger Than Paradise / Paradise Lobby.

---

## 1. Core role

You are **not** presenting publicly as:
- a general creative
- a vague AI artist
- a multi-hyphenate with no center
- a software list pretending to be an identity

You are presenting as:

**AI-first release worldbuilder for music, fashion, and culture-led projects.**

Working definition:
- builds release-ready visual worlds
- combines AI image generation, motion, typography, posters, print studies, and tactile artifact logic
- uses real finishing discipline: Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Premiere, scanning, texture, layout, print prep, and motion structure
- can turn a source image, scene, or concept into a full visual system

---

## 2. Public positioning statement

Primary positioning line:

> I build AI-first release worlds — posters, motion, print studies, and tactile visual systems for artists, brands, and culture-led projects.

Alternative versions:

1. **Release worldbuilder.** AI image systems, motion typography, poster campaigns, and tactile visual outputs.
2. **AI-first visual worldbuilder.** I turn source imagery into campaign-ready worlds across motion, print, and social.
3. **Release systems / posters / motion / print studies.** AI image generation with real graphic finishing and physical proof.

Use one line consistently for at least 60-90 days before changing it.

---

## 3. What this personal brand is NOT

Do not position publicly as:
- generic graphic designer
- generic AI artist
- “I do everything creative”
- social media content creator first
- luxury/polished portfolio brand
- purely Paradise Lobby as the only identity

Reason:
- too broad = forgettable
- too niche = hard to hire
- too cryptic = admired but not commissioned

---

## 4. Hierarchy

### Public hierarchy
1. **Your name / maker identity** = the commercial front door
2. **Stranger Than Paradise** = the larger aesthetic container
3. **Paradise Lobby** = one strict internal department / lab / canon regime

### Rule
Do not make Paradise Lobby the only way new people understand you.
Paradise Lobby proves taste and system-thinking.
Your personal brand wins the work.

## 8. Personal brand pillars

Your public brand should be understood through four pillars.

### Pillar 1 — AI world generation
You generate compelling source imagery and controlled image worlds.

### Pillar 2 — graphic finishing discipline
You do not stop at prompting.
You build layouts, typography, structure, and usable outputs.

### Pillar 3 — motion and sequence
You animate, edit, and turn still systems into short-form campaign material.

### Pillar 4 — tactile proof
You add scanning, texture, resampling, print logic, and physicality.

If a follower cannot see all four over time, the brand is underdeveloped.

## 10. Relationship to Paradise Lobby

Paradise Lobby is not deleted.
It is re-positioned.

### Paradise Lobby should function as
- authority layer
- internal canon
- worldbuilding lab
- proof of discipline
- proof that you think in systems, not single assets

### Paradise Lobby should not function as
- your only public explanation
- your only sales language
- the only aesthetic clients are allowed to understand from you

Paradise Lobby informs the brand.

## 12. Pinned post logic

Three pinned posts are enough.

### Pin 1 — Who you are
A short carousel explaining what kind of work you make.

### Pin 2 — Best proof case
A strong speculative or real release world showing full range.

### Pin 3 — Process-to-finish proof
A carousel or reel showing source -> design -> motion -> print/proof -> final outputs.

---

## 13. Content-to-conversion logic

Every public post must do at least one of the following:
- attract the right followers
- prove you can execute high-level work
- show a recognizable output a client could buy
- strengthen the perceived authority of your system

If a post does none of these, it is not helping the brand.

---

## 14. Voice and language

### Public language should be
- direct
- sparse
- controlled
- confident
- visual-first
- outcome-aware

### Avoid
- overexplaining lore
- apologetic language
- “just experimenting” language
- vague artistic mysticism

---

## 15. Brand promise

What a viewer should come to believe after seeing your account:

> This person can take an image, scene, artist, object, or atmosphere and turn it into a complete visual world that feels publishable, promotable, and hireable.

That is the real brand.

---

## 16. Anti-drift rules

- Do not broaden the brand every week.
- Do not add new labels for yourself constantly.
- Do not lead with software lists.
- Do not make “worldbuilder” mean “I do random things.”
- Do not allow Paradise Lobby to swallow the whole commercial front-end.
- Do not publish work that is impossible for a client to map back to a hireable skill.

---

## 17. 90-day focus statement

For the next 90 days, the personal brand exists to prove one thing:

**You are a hireable AI-first release worldbuilder with strong poster, motion, print, and tactile finishing discipline.**

Everything else is secondary.
